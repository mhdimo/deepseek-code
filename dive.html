1 000 m · midnight zone

The engine is native. The loop is C++.

Beneath the interface, an ai-sdk-cpp Agent owns the whole loop: history, memory injection, sliding-window compaction, retries, and step limits. TypeScript only draws the picture — streaming events become pixels.
